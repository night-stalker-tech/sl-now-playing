# Getting Started

Welcome to **TEKIZMO – SL Now Playing**.

This guide will help you get from your Marketplace delivery to your first use of SL Now Playing.

---

## 1. Unpack SL Now Playing

After receiving SL Now Playing from the Second Life Marketplace, locate the **SL Now Playing Unpacker** in your inventory.

1. Add the Unpacker to your avatar.
2. Click the **UNPACK** button.
3. A new folder named **TEKIZMO - SL Now Playing** will be created in your inventory.
4. After unpacking, the Unpacker will automatically detach.

You can keep the original Marketplace delivery in your inventory in case you need to unpack a fresh copy later.

---

## 2. Add the SL Now Playing HUD

Open the newly created:

**TEKIZMO - SL Now Playing**

folder in your inventory.

Locate the **SL Now Playing HUD** and add it to your avatar.

The HUD is now ready for setup.

---

## 3. Get the PC Application

SL Now Playing uses a companion application on your Windows PC to communicate with the Second Life HUD.

Follow the PC Application guide to download and start the application:

[PC Application Guide](pc-app.md)

---

## 4. Pair Your HUD and PC

Your Second Life HUD must be paired with the SL Now Playing PC application.

Pairing links your HUD to your PC application so they can communicate with each other.

Follow the step-by-step instructions here:

[Pairing Guide](pairing.md)

---

## 5. Start Listening

Once the HUD and PC application are connected, start playing music using a supported player or playback source.

SL Now Playing can then display the current track information in Second Life.

For information about compatible players:

[Supported Players](supported-players.md)
