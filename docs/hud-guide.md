# SL Now Playing HUD Guide

The **SL Now Playing HUD** is the Second Life part of SL Now Playing.

It receives information from the SL Now Playing PC Application and brings your current music information into Second Life.

---

## Attach the HUD

Locate the **SL Now Playing HUD** in your inventory.

Right-click the HUD and select **Add**.

Once attached, the HUD will appear on your screen.

---

## Before Using the HUD

For SL Now Playing to work correctly:

- The SL Now Playing PC Application must be running.
- Your HUD and PC application must be paired.
- A supported media player or playback source must be playing media.

If you have not paired your HUD yet:

[Pairing Guide](pairing.md)

---

## Now Playing

When a supported playback source is detected, SL Now Playing can receive information about the media currently playing on your PC.

Depending on the playback source and the information it provides, available track information may vary.

For information about compatible playback sources:

[Supported Players](supported-players.md)

---

## Download

The HUD provides access to the official **SL Now Playing PC Application** download process.

When you request a download, a temporary personal download page is generated.

For full instructions:

[PC Application Guide](pc-app.md)

---

## Connection

The HUD communicates with your paired SL Now Playing PC Application through the SL Now Playing service.

If the HUD does not receive information from your PC, first make sure:

- The PC application is running.
- Your internet connection is active.
- Your HUD and PC application are correctly paired.
- Media is currently playing from a supported source.

If the problem continues:

[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)

---

## Detaching the HUD

You can detach the SL Now Playing HUD at any time.

Detaching the HUD does not uninstall the PC application.

You can add the HUD again from your Second Life inventory whenever you want to use it.

---

## Fresh Copy

If you accidentally delete or modify your HUD, you can unpack a fresh copy from your original Marketplace delivery.

Marketplace redelivery may also be available for your purchase.
