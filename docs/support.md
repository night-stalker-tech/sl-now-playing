## What to Include

To help us solve your problem as quickly as possible, please include:

- Your Second Life username
- A short description of the problem
- What you were trying to do when the problem occurred
- The media player, browser or playback source you were using
- Any error message displayed by the HUD or PC application
- Your SL Now Playing version, if available

Please **do not include passwords, private access information or other sensitive information** in your support request.

---

## Bug Reports

If you believe you have found a bug, please describe:

1. What you did
2. What you expected to happen
3. What actually happened
4. Whether the problem can be reproduced
5. Which player, browser or playback source was being used

Screenshots can also be helpful when an error message or unexpected HUD behavior is visible.
