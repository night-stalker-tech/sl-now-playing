# SL Now Playing – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 11, 2026**

## 1. Overview

SL Now Playing and the SL Now Playing YouTube Bridge are designed to display information about media currently playing on the user's computer in Second Life.

The YouTube Bridge operates within the user's web browser and communicates with the SL Now Playing desktop application running locally on the user's computer.

## 2. Information Processed

When the user plays content on YouTube, the browser extension may process information necessary for the functionality of SL Now Playing, including:

- Video title
- Channel name
- YouTube video ID and URL
- Playback state, such as playing or paused
- Current playback position and duration
- Playback rate
- Advertisement playback state

The extension accesses this information only on supported YouTube pages and only for the purpose of providing the SL Now Playing functionality.

## 3. Local Communication

The browser extension sends playback information to the SL Now Playing desktop application through a local connection on the user's computer at `127.0.0.1`.

This local communication is used to allow the desktop application to process the currently playing media information and provide the SL Now Playing service.

## 4. Personal Data

SL Now Playing does not intentionally collect personal identification information through the browser extension.

The extension does not collect passwords, payment information, health information, private communications, or authentication credentials.

## 5. Tracking and Analytics

The SL Now Playing YouTube Bridge does not use advertising trackers, analytics services, tracking cookies, or behavioral profiling.

Browsing activity on websites unrelated to the extension's supported functionality is not collected.

## 6. Sale and Sharing of Data

SL Now Playing does not sell user data.

User data is not transferred to third parties for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or purposes unrelated to the functionality of SL Now Playing.

## 7. Data Storage

The browser extension does not maintain a remote database of the user's YouTube playback activity.

Playback information processed by the extension is used to provide the requested functionality and is not retained by the extension as a browsing history.

## 8. Permissions

The extension requests only browser permissions required to provide its functionality.

Access to YouTube pages is used to obtain current playback information. Access to the local address `127.0.0.1` is used to communicate with the SL Now Playing desktop application installed on the user's computer.

## 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of SL Now Playing changes. The current version of the policy will indicate its latest revision date.

## 10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the support contact provided on the official SL Now Playing extension store listing.
