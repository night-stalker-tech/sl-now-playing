# TEKIZMO – SL Now Playing

### Music to your Second Life

**SL Now Playing** connects your music experience on your PC with Second Life.

The SL Now Playing HUD displays information about the music you are currently listening to and provides a simple connection between the SL HUD and the SL Now Playing PC application.
